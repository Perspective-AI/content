Use this template when turning internal wins into public proof.

## Story outline

1. **Context:** company, team, problem (2–3 sentences).
2. **Agent mix:** which of the four agents they deployed + why.
3. **Implementation:** timeline, integrations, stakeholders.
4. **Impact metrics:** conversion lift, interviews/week, drop-off reductions, trust improvements.
5. **Quote or insight:** direct customer pull-quote or anonymized observation.
6. **Artifacts:** highlight page, chart, or video embed.
