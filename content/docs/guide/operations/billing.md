## Plan structure (placeholder)

- **Seats:** Builders/Admins consume paid seats. Analysts/Viewers are free (confirm with finance).
- **Workspaces:** Usage is billed per workspace; consolidate small pilots to avoid surprise invoices.
- **Overages:** Concierge interviews and evaluator scores burn credits; monitor usage dashboard weekly.

## Managing seats

1. Visit **Settings → Billing** in Perspective.
2. Use the seat table to reassign inactive users; export CSV for audits.
3. When inviting new builders, re-use freed seats before contacting sales.

## Invoices & payment

- Invoices are emailed to billing contacts + available under Settings.
- Add AP aliases in-app so finance receives copies.
- For procurement reviews, attach SOC 2/ISO certificates from the compliance packet.

## Forecasting usage

| Metric                        | Where to find it      | Owner        |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------ |
| Active agents per workspace   | Workspace dashboard   | Research ops |
| Concierge conversation volume | Insights → Dashboards | CX ops       |
| Evaluator scoring volume      | Analytics exports     | Product ops  |
