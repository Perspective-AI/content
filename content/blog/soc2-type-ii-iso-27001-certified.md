## SOC 2 Type II. ISO 27001. Done.

*Your conversations are protected. Now it's certified.*

---

People tell voice agents things they'd never type into a form.

The real frustrations. The workarounds. The honest feedback that only comes out when someone feels like they're actually being heard. That depth is what makes conversational AI powerful. It's also what makes security non-negotiable.

We built Perspective AI knowing this from day one. But "we take security seriously" isn't proof. So we got proof.

**Perspective AI is now SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.**

## What this means

**SOC 2 Type II** — the US gold standard for SaaS security. An independent auditor verified our data security, availability, and confidentiality controls over time. Not a snapshot. A sustained audit.

**ISO 27001** — the international standard for information security management. A systematic, independently certified approach to protecting sensitive data.

Together:

- Your customer conversations are protected by independently verified controls
- Your data stays yours — with formalized handling, access, and retention policies
- Your security team gets a SOC 2 report they can actually review
- International compliance requirements are covered

## Why now

We could have waited. Most early-stage companies do.

But voice agents change the equation. A survey stores a checkbox answer. A voice agent captures everything — the hesitation, the offhand comment, the context someone would never type out. That depth demands the highest bar.

Healthcare organizations using Perspective AI for patient intake. Legal firms replacing forms with our concierge. Enterprises running thousands of candid conversations. They need more than a promise.

Now they have a certificate.

## What's in the report

Our SOC 2 Type II report covers:

- **Security** — protection against unauthorized access across infrastructure, application, and data layers
- **Availability** — uptime commitments and platform reliability
- **Confidentiality** — how we handle, store, and restrict access to customer data

Review our security posture and request our SOC 2 report at our [Trust Center](https://app.vanta.com/getperspective.ai/trust/uozs3vvs7i9m917jpclisq).

## What didn't change

Our security practices. We built them in from the start — encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access, penetration testing, workspace isolation, strict access logging.

What changed: an independent third party verified it. That's the point.
